CONTRACT AMENDMENT

This Amendment ("Amendment") is entered into as of January 15, 2025, by and between ABC Corporation, a Delaware corporation ("Company"), and XYZ Services Ltd. ("Contractor").

RECITALS

WHEREAS, the parties entered into a Service Agreement dated June 1, 2024 ("Original Agreement"); and

1. AMENDMENTS

1.1 Section 3.2 of the Original Agreement is hereby amended to read in its entirety as follows:

"The Contractor shall provide monthly reports no later than the 5th business day of each month."

1.2 The compensation set forth in Section 5.1 shall be increased from $10,000 per month to $12,500 per month, effective February 1, 2025.

2. ADDITIONAL TERMS

2.1 All other terms and conditions of the Original Agreement shall remain in full force and effect.

2.2 This Amendment may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original.
